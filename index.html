FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before ordering from Khetan Industries — answered clearly.

Still Have Questions? →
What types of containers do you manufacture?
+
We manufacture a wide range of standard and custom containers including office containers, porta cabins, security guard cabins, toilet cabins, restaurant containers, bunk bed containers, cold storage containers, mobile hospitals, and luxury container offices, all built in-house at our Delhi facility to suit your exact requirements.
What is the price of a porta cabin in Delhi?
+
Pricing depends on the size, material specifications, design, and level of customization you require. Since every project is different, we provide a detailed, transparent quotation after understanding your exact needs. Get in touch with us and our team will guide you through the options and costs involved.
How long does delivery take?
+
Delivery timelines depend on the size, design complexity, and customization required for your order. Once you share your requirements, we'll provide an estimated dispatch schedule. We deliver across PAN India and ensure your container reaches you safely and on schedule.
Can I customize the size, layout, and design?
+
Absolutely! Customization is at the core of what we do. You can choose your preferred size, specify the interior layout, and add features like AC, electrical fittings, windows, partitions, branding, and more. Our design team works closely with you throughout the process to bring your vision to life.
What material is used in your containers?
+
All our containers are built using high-grade MS (Mild Steel) with anti-rust treatment and weatherproof coating. We use certified steel as per industry standards, ensuring excellent structural integrity, durability, and a long service life even in demanding site conditions.
Can containers be stacked or made double-storey?
+
Yes! We manufacture double-storey and stackable container structures with staircase, balcony, and internal partition options, ideal for maximizing space on busy construction sites or corporate campuses. Custom structural engineering is provided for every project to ensure safety and stability.
Do you supply to government and PSU projects?
+
Yes, we regularly supply to government departments, PSUs, defense organizations, and large infrastructure projects across India. We provide GST invoices, quality certificates, and all documentation required for government procurement processes.
